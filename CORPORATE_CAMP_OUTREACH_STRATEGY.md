## 📈 Conversion Strategy: Turning Camps into Patients
Once the HR/Principal approves the camp:
1. **The Event**: Visit the office/school with basic portable dental chairs/lights. Dr. Prem Kumar and team conduct quick 3-minute oral checkups.
2. **The Handout**: Give each participant a "Dental Health Report Card" stating their oral health status (e.g., "Clean, minor scaling needed" or "Cavity detected in lower molar").
3. **The Hook**: Attach a **"Corporate Wellness Privilege Card"** to the report card. This card offers:
   - 15% discount on Root Canals and Dental Implants
   - Complimentary scaling (cleaning) if they book within 14 days
   - 20% discount on pediatric treatments for their kids
4. **The Result**: Employees and parents book appointments at the Attur Main Road clinic for their treatments. You build a direct pipeline of loyal, high-paying local patients.
